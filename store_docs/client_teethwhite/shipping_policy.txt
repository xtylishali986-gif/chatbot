Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $3.99. Orders over $30
ship free with standard shipping automatically applied at checkout.

Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $9.99, available at
checkout for an additional fee.

We currently ship within the country only. International shipping is not
yet available, but we are working on expanding to more regions soon.

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Please
allow up to 24 hours for tracking information to update after receiving
that email.
