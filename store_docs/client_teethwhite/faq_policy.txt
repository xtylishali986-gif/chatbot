Frequently Asked Questions

Is teeth whitening powder safe to use daily?
Our teeth whitening powder is formulated for daily use, but we recommend
starting with 2-3 times per week if you have sensitive teeth, then
increasing frequency as tolerated.

How long does it take to see results?
Most customers notice visible whitening results within 1-2 weeks of
consistent use, though results vary based on individual staining and
usage frequency.

Can I use the whitening foam and powder together?
Yes, many customers alternate between the foam and powder for their daily
routine. We recommend not using both products in the same session.

Are your products safe for sensitive teeth?
Our formulas are designed to be gentle, but if you have known tooth
sensitivity or dental conditions, we recommend consulting your dentist
before starting any whitening routine.
