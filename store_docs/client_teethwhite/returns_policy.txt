Returns Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date for unopened and
unused products. Due to hygiene reasons, opened teeth whitening powder or
foam products cannot be returned or exchanged.

To start a return, email support@yourbrand.com with your order number and
reason for return. We will respond within 1-2 business days with return
instructions.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
after we receive and inspect the returned item.

If you received a damaged or defective product, please email us within 7
days of delivery with photos of the issue. Damaged or defective items are
eligible for a full refund or free replacement at no cost to you, with no
time limit for reporting manufacturing defects.
