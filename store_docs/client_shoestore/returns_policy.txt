Returns Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unworn,
unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

To start a return, contact support@ourstore.com with your order number.
We will email you a prepaid return shipping label within 1 business day.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
after we receive the returned item. Store credit is available instantly upon
request instead of a refund to card.

Final sale items (marked "Final Sale" on the product page) cannot be returned
or exchanged under any circumstances.

Defective or damaged items can be returned at any time, with no time limit,
for a full refund or free replacement.
