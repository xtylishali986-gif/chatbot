Warranty Policy

All footwear comes with a 6-month manufacturer's warranty covering sole
separation, stitching defects, and material failure under normal use.

Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, normal wear and tear, or
water damage on non-waterproof items.

To file a warranty claim, email support@ourstore.com with your order number
and photos of the defect. Approved claims receive a free replacement or
full refund, customer's choice.

Jackets and outerwear carry a 1-year warranty against zipper failure and
seam defects.
