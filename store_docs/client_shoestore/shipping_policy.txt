Shipping Policy

Standard shipping within the country takes 3-5 business days and costs $4.99.
Orders over $75 ship free with standard shipping automatically applied at checkout.

Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99, available at checkout.

We currently ship internationally to Canada, the UK, and the EU only.
International orders take 7-14 business days and customs fees may apply,
which are the responsibility of the customer.

Once an order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. Please
allow 24 hours for tracking information to update after receiving that email.

We do not ship to PO boxes for express shipping orders.
